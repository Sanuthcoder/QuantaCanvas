The Linear Tangent

A Maclaurin series approximates a function $f(x)$ centered at $x=0$. The simplest approximation is a straight line matching the value and slope of the function at that center point.

1 / 5

Adding Curvature

To follow the function's bend, we introduce higher-order derivatives. A cubic term lets the approximation curve gracefully with $f(x)$ as it departs from the center.

1 / 5

Expanding the Horizon

With each added non-zero term, the polynomial gains extra inflection points, enabling it to match the waves of $\sin(x)$ further and further away from zero.

Infinite Playground
